Mossgrid: Sudoku Puzzles

Classic sudoku on a quiet board of moss and river stones. Fill every row, column and box with 1 to 9. Every puzzle has one solution you can reach by logic alone, with no lives, no mistake limit and no ads during a puzzle. Free, plays offline, no account.

Features

  • Every puzzle is checked by a solver that only makes forced moves, so it has one solution and never needs a guess.
  • No lives and no mistake limit: a wrong digit never ends the game. Check rings mistakes when you ask; undo is always free.
  • Pencil notes that tidy themselves, highlighted matching digits, and finished rows and boxes that ripple green.
  • Hints fix a mistake first, otherwise they fill the next cell logic finds.
  • Easy to Expert levels, a Daily Stone for everyone and a 7-day check-in for coins.
  • No ads during a puzzle, no banners; videos for coins are always your choice.
